The "nearly all of it ends in the account the learner keeps" claim is arithmetically true: 2.22 XRP in, 0.2 XRP burned, ~2 XRP ends in the user's kept account.

The friction in one line: **to place a single order on the XRPL DEX you need a wallet app, a seed phrase, an exchange account with KYC to buy XRP, a withdrawal, and 1 XRP locked as reserve.** Tide's funded onboarding removes all five steps for the first account.
