# Tide, pitch video script (max 180 s, vertical, two speakers)

Read to camera, phone held vertical, the app open behind you or cut in over the voice. About 400 words: around 160 seconds at a normal pace. Swap the speaker labels however you like.

---

**Eli.** Hi, we are Tide. Tide is a free trading school built on the XRP Ledger, live on mainnet today at tidetrade dot xyz.

**Armand.** The problem: most people learn to trade by losing real money. Seventy-four to eighty-nine percent of retail accounts lose. Last year, a hundred and fifty billion dollars of crypto positions were liquidated, mostly by people nobody taught.

**Eli.** And every place to practise is a dead end. A stock game ends at a score. An exchange demo ends at "now deposit". Every XRPL app starts at "connect a funded wallet". Nobody teaches first.

**Armand.** So we built the school first. Sixteen lessons and a guided tutorial on live prices: you open a ten-x perp, get liquidated with fake money, and learn the rule that keeps you alive: never risk more than one percent on one idea. No account, no wallet, nothing to pay.

**Eli.** Then you prove it. Ten thousand virtual dollars on real prices, a leaderboard, competitions with on-chain tickets and prizes paid in XRP. Your first trade becomes a soulbound NFT. A track record you own.

**Armand.** And here is what matters to XRPL. Every simulator stops at the simulation. Tide's ends on the ledger. Every learner gets a real XRPL account on their first click. We create it, encrypt the key, and fund the reserve ourselves: about one point two XRP, a dollar fifty-five, and it stays in their balance.

**Eli.** Why do we pay for it? Because our users come from a lesson, not from an exchange. Asking a beginner to install a wallet, save a seed phrase, pass KYC, buy XRP and lock a reserve before lesson one is a paywall on a free school. Nothing touches the ledger before the human trades. Every learner becomes an XRPL account, earned by learning, not bought.

**Armand.** When they are ready, the same terminal flips to Live and signs a real swap on the native DEX, XRP against RLUSD, non-custodial, every transaction tagged. Tide takes no fee on swaps.

**Eli.** The XRP Ledger has the exchange, and about eleven hundred accounts trading on it a day. The rails are there. The crowd is not.

**Armand.** Next: an AI coach, weekly leagues, Tide for classrooms. Then funded traders, under the risk rules that already run on our AI agents. Then the venue.

**Eli.** The XRP Ledger has the exchange. Tide brings the traders.

**Armand.** Learn to trade. Prove it. Get funded. On the ledger.

**Both.** Tide. Live on mainnet. tidetrade dot xyz.
