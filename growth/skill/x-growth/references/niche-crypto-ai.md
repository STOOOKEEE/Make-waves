The *code* survived (ElizaOS still developed, ~18.3k GitHub stars). The *token thesis* did not.

**What replaced it:** prediction markets (a single World Cup final drove ~$2B), RWA/tokenization, and
stablecoins — capital rotating toward things with real revenue.

### But the format is validated

**Nof1's Alpha Arena** (launched 2025-10-17): LLMs competing head-to-head on a live public leaderboard.
It went viral, ran a second season, spawned Polymarket markets on its outcomes, and spawned direct
competitors (Recall Network's Trading Arena, rallies.ai). The founder announced humans would join
future seasons. `[PRACTITIONER-CONSENSUS on virality; WELL-EVIDENCED that it exists and recurred]`

> **Tide's format is validated. Tide's category label is contaminated.**

### The positioning consequence

**Lead with arena / competition / leaderboard. Let "AI agent" be *how it works*, not *what it is*.**
"AI agent" as a **category claim** now carries the smell of a 99% drawdown to everyone who was there.
Alpha Arena's traction came from the **spectacle of models publicly competing and losing** — not from
an agent thesis. That spectacle is exactly what `growth/context/04-content-engine.md` series 1 and 5 are built
on.

**And a useful constraint:** *"AI bots auditioning for Wall Street trading are mostly losing money."*
Perfect for us — an arena profits from competition, not from agents winning. But it means **never
imply agents make money.** See `growth/context/05-facts.md`.

**Do not launch a token. Do not run an airdrop.**

## 2. MCP is table stakes, not a differentiator

`[WELL-EVIDENCED]`

- **~9,400 tracked MCP servers** by mid-April 2026, **+38% in four months**.
- The **2026-07-28 spec** is the largest revision since launch — stateless, commodity HTTP, plus
  Enterprise-Managed Authorization adopted by Anthropic, Microsoft, and Okta.
- **X itself ships an official hosted MCP server** (`docs.x.com/tools/mcp`). When the platform you're
  marketing on has one, MCP is infrastructure.

Ecosystem consensus, verbatim: *"MCP support becomes table stakes for any serious AI host, not a
differentiator"*; *"MCP commoditizes the integration layer."*

**"MCP-native" is worth one clause in a bio** as a compatibility signal to the Claude Desktop / Cursor
crowd. **Never a headline.** Leading with it signals not knowing the space, to the exact audience most
likely to notice.

**What actually differentiates us:**

1. **Humans and AI agents on one leaderboard.** Alpha Arena is bots-only. We're the version where your
   homemade bot has to beat actual people, and vice versa. Nobody else is running that, and it's the
   funnier product.
2. **Nothing is real, so anyone can play.** No deposit, no KYC, no money. The barrier to entry is zero
   and the barrier to *distrust* is zero — see §3.
3. **The agent mandate.** You set hard limits and the server enforces them before every trade; the bot
   cannot argue past them. Good material precisely because bots keep *trying* (`growth/context/04-content-engine.md`
   series 1) — not because it's an enterprise feature.

⚠️ What MCP content performs on X: **could not be measured** (no X API access this pass). Don't let
anyone tell you otherwise without data.

## 3. We're in a bear market and a scam wave. That's an advantage.

`[WELL-EVIDENCED]`

Crypto is ~48% off peak, communities are demoralized, and there's an active influx of scam projects
across the ecosystem. Any new crypto project promising trading returns via AI agents pattern-matches
almost perfectly to the scam template this audience has been trained to attack. **You are assumed
guilty until proven otherwise.**

**Which is exactly why paper-only is the strongest possible position right now:**

> There is no deposit. There is no wallet. There is no money. There is nothing to rug.

That's the cleanest answer to "is this a scam?" that a crypto product can give in 2026, and it costs
us nothing because it's just true. **Lead with it.** "no deposit, no kyc, no reason not to"
(`growth/context/02-voice.md`) is doing double duty as a growth line and as a trust signal.

**What else defuses the suspicion:**
- **Ship-first, receipts-always.** Real leaderboards, real screenshots, the hackathon affiliation.
- **Never post P&L or price-call content.** The moment we post a P&L screenshot framed as a result, we
  are indistinguishable from a signal-group scammer (`guardrails.md` §4–5).
- **No token, no airdrop.** In this market, that *is* the scam tell.
- **Keep TIDE distinct from Make Waves.** Hackathon affiliation is credibility; implying a Ripple
  endorsement gets us publicly corrected, fast.

## 4. Launch mechanics — what's dead, what works

### ⭐ InfoFi / yap-to-earn is structurally over `[WELL-EVIDENCED]`

On **2026-01-15, X banned apps that financially incentivize posting** and **revoked programmatic API
access** from affected developers, citing *"AI slop & reply spam."* **Kaito sunset Yaps entirely**
(a ~157,000-member community); KAITO fell ~17%. Founder Yu Hu: *"fully permissionless,
incentive-driven distribution models are no longer viable under current platform constraints."*

**→ Kaito/InfoFi is not a channel. It no longer exists.** And **never build a Tide mechanic that pays
people to post** — platform-policy violation, on top of the engagement-solicitation rule
(`guardrails.md` §1).

### Points / airdrops — broadly spent `[PRACTITIONER-CONSENSUS]`
Farmers dump on receipt; Berachain's TVL fell ~90% post-launch as emissions dried. The counter-example
is **Hyperliquid**, which won on genuine usage and distributed to real users. ⚠️ Secondary sources, no
methodology — directional only.

### KOL deals — no credible data exists `[FOLKLORE]`
**Every source found on crypto KOL ROI was a marketing agency selling KOL services.** There is no
independent ROI data. Anyone quoting a crypto-KOL conversion benchmark is improvising. Treat rate
cards as vendor pricing, not evidence. (Paid promotion also carries disclosure duties — `guardrails.md`.)

### Trading competitions as acquisition — proven `[WELL-EVIDENCED]`
TradingView, Kraken, Crypto.com and WhiteBIT all run them. **This is our format**, and ours is the
free version, which removes the friction all of theirs have.

### ⚠️ Our real competitive set is prop firms, not Investopedia `[C]`
The fastest-growing youth-facing segment in trading is **prop-firm challenges** — free-entry
competition as top of funnel, marketed through Discord and trader-founder authenticity, at scale with
a young audience. Their "funded" accounts are usually **still simulated**: the prop industry is
essentially paid paper trading with a payout wrapper, and it is enormous.

**Consequences:** (1) paper trading is **not** an unclaimed category — never claim we're first;
(2) free-competition-as-acquisition is a *proven* template, so copy it rather than inventing one;
(3) the youth demand for simulated trading is already demonstrated, which is good news for fit.

## 4b. ⚠️ The timing risk — read before writing meme content

In **May 2026** a major outlet ran *"Gen Z's Joe Camel moment: how prediction markets learned to speak
in memes"* — documenting Kalshi/Polymarket using **exactly the playbook we're about to use**:
unhinged humor, absurdist ads, a meme vendor whose founder says on record *"if you want to attract a
younger audience, you're going to use memes… unhinged humor."* The piece runs that next to **69% of
one platform's users losing money across 588M trades**, 100K+ accounts down $1,000+, and quotes
addiction researchers on "highly stimulating content plus frictionless access."

**The "unhinged memes aimed at young traders" playbook is, as of two months ago, the thing being
written up as the next Joe Camel.**

Our defence is real and it's the strongest one available: **nobody can lose anything here.** But it
only works if it's the loudest thing we say. If our memes out-run our "none of this is real" message,
we inherit the entire backlash — without even having their revenue model.

→ Operationally: the no-real-money fact belongs in the bio, the pinned post, and near every number.
Not as a disclaimer. As the pitch. See `growth/context/02-voice.md` hard limits.

## 5. What to actually do

1. **The arena is the growth mechanic.** Alpha Arena proved a public agent leaderboard generates
   organic attention with **zero incentive spend**. Make the leaderboard public, embeddable,
   screenshot-able — and **show the denominator** (losses and mid-table, not just winners:
   `guardrails.md` §5). The losses are better content anyway.
2. **Humans vs bots is the ownable angle.** It's the one thing in this space nobody else has, and it's
   inherently a story rather than a feature.
3. **Reply-led growth** (`replies.md`) — no budget, `[CODE]`-grade mechanics, immune to the InfoFi
   crackdown.
4. **Free paper competitions as the whole funnel** — zero friction, strongest anti-scam signal
   available in a scam-wary market.
5. **Skip:** token, airdrop, points program, yap campaigns, paid KOLs.
