# Posted — the archive

**Check this before drafting.** It's the only defence against repeating ourselves, and repetition is
invisible from the inside.

Newest first. Leave performance blank until someone reads the real numbers — never estimate them.

Format:
```
### YYYY-MM-DD · series · [link]
> the post

perf: likes / replies / QTs / profile visits — leave blank if unread
notes: anything worth remembering
```

Account: **[@tidetradexyz](https://x.com/tidetradexyz)** — post links look like
`https://x.com/tidetradexyz/status/<id>`.

---

⚠️ **This archive starts empty, but the account may not be.** Nobody has checked what's already on
[@tidetradexyz](https://x.com/tidetradexyz) — X blocks automated reads, so it needs a human or a
browser session. **Backfill anything already posted before drafting new material**, otherwise the
staleness check is running against a false blank slate.

> ✅ **URL policy, decided 2026-08-01: stop chasing links.** Eli posts straight from X and the URLs
> don't get captured. That's the real workflow, so the archive records **date + series + full text**
> and nothing else. **This costs less than it looks:** X's own analytics lists posts with their
> numbers, and the full text here is enough to match a row to an entry. Performance still gets filled
> in; it just gets read from the analytics screen instead of followed from a link.
