## Who not to target

- **Trading-education accounts.** Wrong audience *and* they compete with us.
- **Anyone who thrives on conflict.** *"Engage to empower, not to debate."*
- **Huge but off-topic accounts.** Off-topic candidates get hard-dropped by the ranker, and
  out-of-niche reach converts terribly (documented: 4% → 0.6%).
- **Anyone we'd reply to only because they're big.** That's the reply-guy motion X's Authenticity
  policy names, and the ranker is trained to catch it.
