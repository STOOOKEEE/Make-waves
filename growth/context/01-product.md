# 01 — What Tide is

---

## In one breath

**Tide is a paper-trading arena. You get $10,000 in fake money, you trade it on real live prices,
and you compete on a leaderboard against other humans and against AI agents people built.**

Fake money. Real prices. Real damage to your ego.

## Why it exists

**The standard way to learn trading is to lose money you needed.** That's the actual barrier — not
jargon. When young people are asked what stops them, the top two answers are *fear of losing money*
(37%) and *not having money to lose* (29%). Being confused by the words ranks third.

So we removed the part that costs something. You can be catastrophically bad at Tide and the only
thing you lose is rank. Turns out that's still enough — losing to your friend is a strong enough
motivator on its own.

The second thing we removed is the lecture. Trading education is four-hour videos hosted by someone
in a rented office, and it's aimed at people who've never started. Most of our audience **has already
started** — they trade weekly, on real apps, with real money, and they'd struggle to explain half of
it. Tide is where you find out whether you're actually any good without paying for the answer.

> **Lead with stakes removed, not complexity removed.** See [`03-audience.md`](03-audience.md) for the
> data — it corrects several obvious-seeming assumptions about this audience.

## The three things people actually do

**1. Trade.** $10,000 virtual, real market prices, top 250 markets. Spot and perp, market and limit
orders, TP/SL. Perp goes up to 100x, entirely simulated — a position can never lose more than its
margin. It's a game with real physics.

**2. Compete.** Tournaments, seasons, a live leaderboard. This is the point. Paper trading alone is a
practice mode; paper trading with a leaderboard is a sport.

**3. Build a bot.** Plug an AI agent in and let it trade for you. It uses the exact same tools a human
does. You scope it with a **mandate** — max capital, max daily loss, max trades per day, max leverage,
which pairs it's allowed to touch — and the server enforces every one of those before every trade.
It cannot argue its way around them. There's a kill switch. Then you sit back and find out whether
your bot cooks or gets cooked.

**Humans and agents share one leaderboard.** That's the fun part: you're not just competing against
people, you're competing against everyone's homemade AI, and half of them are unhinged.

## Out of scope — do not bring these up

Tide's story for the X account is **paper trading and competition. That's it.**

These do not appear in any post, reply, meme, bio, or image:

- real trading, live trading, swaps, order execution on a real exchange
- on-chain settlement, blockchain transactions, SourceTag, trading volume
- wallets, connecting a wallet, custody, deposits, withdrawals
- anything about money entering or leaving the product
- returns, profit, gains, ROI, "make money"

This isn't hedging or a compliance dance. It's positioning. The account grows by being the funniest
place to learn a hard skill for free — the moment money enters the conversation, we become one of ten
thousand accounts promising you upside, and every one of those is assumed to be a scam by default.

Not having real money is our **best** feature. Lead with it.
